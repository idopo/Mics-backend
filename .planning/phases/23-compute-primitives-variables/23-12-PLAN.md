<backend_decision>
**CMP-25 unions semantic hardware at the call site, and does NOT widen `_valid_flag_names`.**
`23-CONTEXT.md` left this to the planner; here is the decision and its evidence, so the
executor does not re-open it.

`_valid_flag_names` (`fda_validation.py:310-316`) is called from **two** places:
- `:96` — `validate_trigger_assignments`, whose `valid_names` then flows into `_validate_action`
- `:245` — `validate_compute_variables`, the condition-operand check CMP-25 is about

Inside `_validate_action`, that same set gates **three write-side rules**:
- `:408-410` a `type:"flag"` action's `ref` ("flag '<ref>' not declared")
- `:426-431` an `output` capture slot
- `:417-420` a `key_template` token

All three resolve against `self.flags` on the Pi. A semantic hardware name is **not** in
`self.flags` — `{"type":"flag","ref":"<device>"}` would `KeyError` at FDA load. Widening
`_valid_flag_names` would therefore make three genuinely-invalid constructs save cleanly and
fail on the rig — trading one bad 422 for three bad successes. **Union at `:245` only.**

This is the same read-vs-write split the whole phase is built on: `view` reads a superset,
`flag` writes a subset.
</backend_decision>
