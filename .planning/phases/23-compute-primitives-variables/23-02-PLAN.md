<objective>
Build the storage substrate: one new column (`hardware_libs.kind`), one new version field
(`declared_imports`), the seed compute lib and its registration, and the auto-provisioning of
the per-pilot config row a compute module needs before it will instantiate on the Pi.

Purpose: CONTEXT's reframe is "a compute lib IS a hardware lib." Research proved that reusing
the substrate means reusing the FULL hardware-module ceremony — `hardware_modules` row +
toolkit membership + a per-pilot `pilot_hardware_config` row — not just
`toolkit_hardware_libs`. Without the config row, `init_hardware()` hits a KeyError, silently
logs and skips, and any `compute` action dies later at FDA-load with a bare KeyError on
`self._semantic_hw[ref]`. The user's locked decision is to auto-provision that row.
Output: a compute lib can be uploaded, seeded, registered as a module and made reachable on
every pilot, with zero Pi-side change.
</objective>
