<the_boot_time_mkdir_constraint>
`autopilot/autopilot/prefs.py:478-484` `mkdir`s **every** pref of scope `Scopes.DIRECTORY` at
boot. That is why `pilot/{data,logs}` "keep their paths": you may empty them, but the pref values
must remain real paths, and the directories must exist in the repo so a fresh clone does not
depend on that mkdir succeeding.

So: `.gitkeep` + `.gitignore`, **never** delete the directory or blank the pref.
`DATADIR`, `LOGDIR`, `CALIBRATIONDIR`, `PROTOCOLDIR`, `SOUNDDIR`, `VIZDIR`, `PLUGINDIR` are all
in this family — check each one's scope in `prefs.py` before changing its value.

**`pilot/sounds/` is untouched.** An unreferenced-looking wav can still be a live cue: `mixer.py`
resolves by bare filename through `SOUNDDIR`, so static analysis cannot prove a wav dead.
</the_boot_time_mkdir_constraint>
