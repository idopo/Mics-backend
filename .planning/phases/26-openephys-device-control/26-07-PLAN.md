**`liveness_ok(status, run_active)` is a PURE decision, in the same family as `decide_start` and
`is_already_recording` — keep it here, not in the seeded lib.** `api/seed_libs/openephys.py` imports
`ExternalHardware` and therefore `autopilot`, so nothing in it can be unit-tested on the dev host;
factoring the decision out is what makes plan 26-10's `liveness_hook` provable by an agent instead
of only at the rig. Rules: `status is None` (the GET failed) → False. `run_active` False → True,
since outside a run reachability alone is liveness. `run_active` True → True iff the status still
reports `RECORD` (reuse `is_already_recording`). That second clause is what implements
`26-CONTEXT.md`'s locked *"recording stops mid-run → flip `alive`"* decision.

**`make_client(timeout_s)` is load-bearing for plan 26-10, not a convenience.** `OpenEphys`'s
`liveness_hook` polls `get_status` on a `LivenessPoller` daemon thread at `stale_ms / 2`; at the
canonical `stale_ms: 3000` that is every 1.5 s, so the module-wide `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_S = 5.0` would
stack calls and outlive the bounded join in `LivenessPoller.stop()`. Export the factory rather than
leaving callers to reach into a private `_default_client` helper or hand-roll their own transport —
this module exists precisely to centralize the transport. Its `timeout_s` must reach the underlying
client's connect AND read timeouts, not just one of them; an unreachable host hangs on connect.

**Python 3.7.3 constraint.** The rig runs 3.7.3. No f-string `=` specifier, no walrus in a
comprehension, no `dict |` merge, no `list[str]` builtin generics in annotations — use `typing`
or omit annotations. Anything newer imports fine on the dev host and explodes on the rig, which is
the worst possible place to find out.
