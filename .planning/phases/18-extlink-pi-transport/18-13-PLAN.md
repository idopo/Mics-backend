<objective>
Make an `ExternalHardware` signal a first-class, *offerable* view key on the backend: aggregate
`ast_metadata.extlink` (plan 18-07) against per-pilot `pilot_hardware_config.config` into
`<source_id>.<signal>` keys, surface them on the toolkit read, and teach the two gates that
currently reject such a key — the save-time 422 and preflight's view-key resolution.

Purpose: EXTLINK-05 says an FDA reads an external signal through the ordinary
`view.get_value("dlc_cam1.left_paw_x")` call site. Today the backend has no idea that key exists.
`fda_validation.validate_compute_variables` **hard-rejects it at save with 422**
(`references unknown variable/flag 'dlc_cam1.left_paw_x'`) and, if it somehow got saved,
`resolve_view_key_issues` reports it as `view_key_unresolved` at preflight. So the phase's own
ROADMAP success criterion 1 is currently unreachable through any supported path. This plan closes
that, and is also what plan 18-14's editor picker consumes.
Output: one new `api/extlink_keys.py`, its test file, and three surgical wirings.
</objective>

<design_decision>
**The stored operand shape is the RESOLVED key — `{"view": "<source_id>.<signal>"}` — not a ref.**
Plan 18-14 carries the full rationale (it is the plan that authors the operand); this plan is
written against that decision and must not invent a second shape. The two consequences that land
HERE are the only reason this plan exists:

1. A resolved key must pass the **save-time** gate. `validate_compute_variables` (called from
   `collect_hard_errors` -> `reject_if_hard_errors`) hard-errors on any `{"view": name}` whose
   `name` is not a toolkit flag, declared variable, `trial_counter`, module name, detector-derived
   key, or semantic-hardware name. An extlink key is none of those. **Verify this yourself before
   writing code** — save a condition reading `dlc_cam1.left_paw_x` through
   `PUT /api/task-definitions/{id}` and paste the 422 into the SUMMARY. That failing call is this
   plan's RED.
2. A resolved key is pilot-specific in its `source_id` half, so the cross-pilot risk DVK-11 exists
   to prevent is real here. It is answered at **preflight**, not at run time: adding the target
   pilot's extlink keys to `valid_keys` makes a key naming another pilot's `source_id` surface as
   the EXISTING `view_key_unresolved` issue. **No new issue kind** — plan 18-08 already owns the
   two this phase adds (`device_held`, `extlink_config_invalid`) and `PREFLIGHT_ISSUE_KINDS` must
   not grow a third here.
</design_decision>
