# On-cue vs off-cue, lick density, and ITI timing

licks in 1s after reward: 7.80 vs after off-cue poke: 3.28 (reward = 2.4x denser)
ITI (from disengagement) off-cue pokes  early:late = 7:8 (more LATE (anticipatory))
